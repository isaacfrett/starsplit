One star. Four ways to break it.

A cosmic match-3 built around a single move. Every level hands you one star. Tap it — don't swap it — and you choose the shape it breaks into.

  • Cross

    Reach

  • Rows

    Sweep

  • Columns

    A lane

  • Crater

    Concentration

They are deliberately not a power ladder. On a nine-wide board they land within a handful of cells of each other. They differ in shape, not size — so the pick is a real one, and it belongs to the level in front of you rather than to a best answer you memorise once.

Three hundred levels, each one measured

Every level is played thousands of times by a bot before it ships, and its move budget is set from what that measures rather than from a guess. A level that turns out to be unfair is a level that can be found and fixed — level 187 is the same board for every player on every device.

Built to be read on a small screen

Colourblind marks, a reduce-motion setting that shortcuts every flourish, and separate toggles for sound, music and haptics. The board sizes itself to your device rather than sitting in a letterboxed rectangle in the middle of it.
