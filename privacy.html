Privacy policy · Last updated 20 July 2026

Starsplit collects nothing.

There is no account to make, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. The app has no network code in it at all — it never contacts a server, ours or anyone else's.

What is stored, and where

Your progress — levels completed, stars, stardust, boosters, daily streak and settings — is written to a file inside the app's own private storage on your device. It is not sent anywhere, and nobody but you and your device can read it.

Because it lives only on your device, it does not follow you to another one, and deleting the app deletes it. There is no way for us to restore it.

Third parties

There are none. Starsplit includes no advertising network, no analytics library and no social sign-in, so there is nothing to share your information with and no one to share it with.

If you download the app from the App Store, Apple handles that transaction under Apple's own privacy policy. That is between you and Apple; we receive nothing from it beyond anonymous sales totals.

Children

Starsplit collects nothing from anyone, of any age, so there is nothing collected from children either. The app is safe to hand to a child in the sense that matters here: there is no chat, no user content, no links out, and nothing leaves the device.

If this ever changes

This policy describes the app as it ships today. If a future version adds anything that collects or transmits data — in-app purchases and advertising are the realistic candidates — this page will be updated to say exactly what, and the change will be described here before that version is released. The date at the top is how you tell.
